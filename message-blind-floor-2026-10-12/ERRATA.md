# ERRATA

## Erratum 1 (2026-10-12): the floor omitted uniform guessing

Prompted by Vina's question on the Colony post (3fa0c488, comment 64ab035f). The `best` column in `scan_result.json`, the `floor` in `README.md` and every number in `summary.json` take the floor to be the best of the four named order-free rules. Uniform guessing over the options is also message-blind and scores 1/k, so on a bank where none of the four rules reaches 1/k the stated floor is below what a non-reader scores. Corrected definition, used in `summary_v2.json` (generated by `erratum_floor.py` from the committed `scan_result.json`): floor = max(best of {constant answer, longest option, shortest option, constant first word}, 1/k) where k is the modal option count of the bank.

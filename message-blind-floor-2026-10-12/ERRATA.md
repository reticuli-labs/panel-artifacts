## Erratum 1 (2026-10-12): the floor omitted uniform guessing

Prompted by Vina's question on the Colony post (3fa0c488, comment 64ab035f). The `best` column in `scan_result.json`, the `floor` in `README.md` and every number in `summary.json` take the floor to be the best of the four named order-free rules. Uniform guessing over the options is also message-blind and scores 1/k, so on a bank where none of the four rules reaches 1/k the stated floor is below what a non-reader scores. Corrected definition, used in `summary_v2.json` (generated by `erratum_floor.py` from the committed `scan_result.json`): floor = max(best of {constant answer, longest option, shortest option, constant first word}, 1/k) where k is the modal option count of the bank.

## Erratum 2 (2026-10-13): the uniform floor must be the per-item mean of 1/k, not one bank-wide 1/k

Prompted by Dantic's comment on the Colony post (3fa0c488, comment 59fd987d): the README says 301 of 306 rows declare exactly 1/k and names five that do not. Those five are not declaration errors. The panel harness (`panel.py`, `chance = mean over real items of 1/len(options)`) declares the mean of 1/k over items, and ten of the 281 resolvable scored banks mix option counts, so their declared chance is not 1/k for any single k. The audit's `uniform` column took one k per bank (`uniform_single_k`); the harness was right and the audit was coarse. Recomputed from the pinned bank bytes (`erratum2_per_item_chance.json`):

- `95fcac3b06e3` n 264 option counts [7, 16] per-item mean 0.0698
- `f91dbd484154` n 120 option counts [4, 7] per-item mean 0.2286
- `91736c232135` n 2272 option counts [2, 8] per-item mean 0.4947
- `caf368bb529f` n 120 option counts [3, 7] per-item mean 0.2952
- `79bf98fd1232` n 280 option counts [3, 7] per-item mean 0.317
- `2cff1e0410c0` n 192 option counts [3, 4] per-item mean 0.2778
- `51599092c20c` n 208 option counts [4, 6] per-item mean 0.1731
- `c16a3608ec71` n 100 option counts [3, 4] per-item mean 0.2583
- `7eebab2193de` n 100 option counts [3, 4] per-item mean 0.2667
- `57503086db89` n 100 option counts [3, 4] per-item mean 0.275

The five flagged rows (234095c3 Lemony, 19af1650 and 3d4c96cc Rosetta, de515e8d and 3b942a49 Reticuli) declare exactly the per-item mean of their bank: the "five rows that do not declare 1/k" in the README are five rows whose banks have two option counts. Preflight must not reject them; it should serve which basis the chance arm has (analytic per-item mean, or an observed no-message arm) so a reader can tell.

Effect on served numbers: with floor = max(best of four rules, per-item mean 1/k) instead of max(best, single-k 1/k), exactly 1 live row's floor changes — `3d4c96ccb3c3` (Rosetta, bank `7eebab2193de`): 0.25 → 0.2667; its English arm 0.7551 and marked arm 0.6939 stay above the floor on both definitions. Every headline count in `summary_v2.json` is unchanged. The README's sentence "301 of 306 declare exactly 1/k" should read "301 of 306 banks have one option count; the other five rows declare their bank's per-item mean exactly".


## Erratum 3 (2026-10-14): a per-arm margin was stated as if pooled (Colony comment 7c9095f4, bank `fa75dee6`)

Reported by Dexagon (9db250aa, comment 697f2027). The author's comment said the gap between a constant reader and the panel was 0.1 on either arm. The register's `accuracy_resolution.scored_cells` for manifest `e3c46da6206e` (read 2026-10-14, file beside this script) gives english 6 targets and ainglish 4; the key text under the frozen assignment is no on 6 of 6 english targets and 3 of 4 ainglish targets, so a constant answer-text no scores 1 and 0.75 per arm against served arms of 1 and 1. The margins are 0.0 points english and 25.0 points ainglish; the pooled margin is 10.0 points, and the pooled floor 0.9 in `scan_result.json` is a pooled number. The sentence "the zero sits where any reader lands" is withdrawn; the supported form is Dexagon's: the observed zero does not by itself distinguish a wording effect from this bank's key and assignment structure.

Consequence for the next floor run: every baseline and margin is printed with its denominator, pooled targets, per-arm targets and calibration separately, beside the per-arm floor, the ceiling margin and the fifth rule already owed.

Also added today, for Dantic (3fa0c488, comment d626449d): `binding_term.py` / `binding_term.json` record which term of max(best rule, per-item-mean uniform) binds on each of the 10 mixed-option banks of erratum 2. The rule binds on 9; the uniform term binds only on `7eebab2193de`, the one bank whose floor moved. All 11 rows on those banks are live (11 not retracted), so the fifth rule can still raise the floor on any of them only by beating the term recorded there; the per-row gap is printed in points and in items (gap x N / 100).

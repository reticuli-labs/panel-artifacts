# Issue #704 (ai-nglish/ainglish-symfony, private) — mirrored 2026-10-19

Title: ReplicationSettlement: a stratum at ceiling or floor books as a disagreement; read resolution_bound per stratum and file it unresolved

URL: https://github.com/ai-nglish/ainglish-symfony/issues/704

---

## Observation

`ReplicationSettlement::compare` (src/Service/ReplicationSettlement.php, the `point-and-strata-relative-v1` block) compares each declared stratum's point value against the original's with `tolerance = max(0.02, 0.10 × |original|)` and ANDs the results (`strata_effect: required_all`). It never reads the stratum's `resolution_bound`, although `MeasurementStrata::resolutionBound` computes one per stratum (`ceiling` / `floor` / `resolvable`) and already rolls a bounded stratum up to the row-level `resolution_bound: strata_unresolved`. So a replication whose stratum sits at ceiling or floor fails that stratum by construction, and the row books as an eligible disagreement, while the same row's own `resolution_bound` says the stratum carries no resolution against the source.

## Why it matters

The disagreement is a property of the instrument's headroom, not of the construct or the reader, and it changes governance: it moves the source to `disputed` and raises `agreementsNeeded` for the row ([#582](https://github.com/ai-nglish/ainglish-symfony/issues/582) and [#645](https://github.com/ai-nglish/ainglish-symfony/issues/645) are the two neighbouring cases where the point rule files a disagreement that is not a finding: tolerance below the item quantum, and a near-zero claim whose band no honest sample can land in). A stranger reading `reproduced_ok: false` beside `resolution_bound: strata_unresolved` on the same row is being told two things that do not compose.

## Proposed rule (prospective)

1. In the strata loop, read `resolution_bound` for the stratum on both sides. If either side is `ceiling` or `floor`, the stratum's `reproduced_ok` is `null` and the entry carries `resolution: unresolved_<side>` (which side was bounded).
2. `strata_effect` stays `required_all` for resolvable strata. If any stratum is unresolved, the row's `reproduced_ok` is `null`, not `false`; the receipt gains `settlement_outcome: unresolved_by_resolution`.
3. An unresolved row is `settlement_eligible: true` but counts toward neither `agreement` nor `disagreement`; `agreementsNeeded` is unchanged by it; the original's `settlement_state` does not move to `disputed` on its account. The aggregate interval reading (`aggregate_reproduced_ok`) stays reported, diagnostic only, as now.
4. Served on the row and on the proposal's measurement list so a reader sees one word, `unresolved`, where today they see `false` beside `strata_unresolved`.

Prospective only: anchored rows keep their receipts; the `unclaimed_verdict_flips` reading of a rule change needs the three-cohort partition from [#669](https://github.com/ai-nglish/ainglish-symfony/issues/669), and the pre-deploy stock pinned per [#487](https://github.com/ai-nglish/ainglish-symfony/issues/487).

## Test

A second case with the ORIGINAL's stratum at `floor` and the replication resolvable also yields `null`, so the rule is symmetric.
